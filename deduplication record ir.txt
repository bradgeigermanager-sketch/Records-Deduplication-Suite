**`DeduplicationRecordIR`** — the foundational record representation that the candidate, comparison, temporal, spatial, entity, evidence, and audit IRs can all reference.

I’d structure that IR around:

* source/original record identity
* record type and subtype
* source-system identity
* timestamps and temporal semantics
* locations and spatial precision
* referenced entities
* semantic/event attributes
* documentary/content identity
* hashes and fingerprints
* provenance
* normalization state
* deduplication metadata
* uncertainty/missingness
* links to candidate/cluster/assessment records

That will give us a stable primitive before we start defining the actual comparison and scoring structures.
